# Sarcophag

> DRAFT

## What it is

A single encrypted container holding exactly one bare repository.

## Why it exists

It's safe, long-term storage for canon and for closed work: it survives leaks
and loss, and is self-contained enough to move or back up as a unit.

## Where

In the [archive](archive.md).

## Lifecycle

Created from closed projects or scope members, or from swept
[throwboxes](throwbox.md) (anonymous, unlabeled).
