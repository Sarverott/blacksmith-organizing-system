# Throwbox

> DRAFT

## What it is

An automated collector of various loose files related to one project set.
Every scope has one, and the forge has a periodic one.

## Why it exists

Loose, uncategorized files appear in every workspace. A throwbox gives them a
place, so they don't clutter real artefacts, and nothing gets lost.

## Lifecycle

When its scope closes, BOS sweeps the throwbox: it auto-describes what was
captured and packs random sets. Abandoned, uncategorized content becomes an
anonymous, unlabeled [sarcophag](sarcophag.md) hooked to the scope, kept for
final sorting, possible reuse or a later decision to delete.
